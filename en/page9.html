Stella de Ma

Experience the charm of ancient Noli in a typical local house overlooking the cathedral square. Everything is a few steps away, especially the sea.

Previous Next

Features

Description

A typical, renovated medieval Noli house.

The apartment is on the second floor in a historic caruggiu (narrow alley) of the old town, with views of the cathedral square, the old town and the castle. It comprises:
- Living room with dining table and fully equipped kitchen
- Bedroom with 1 double bed, 1 single bed, wardrobe, chest of drawers, TV and ceiling fan
- Bathroom with washbasin, shower, toilet and bidet
- Storage room

Surroundings

Located in a typical caruggiu (narrow alley) of the old town, less than 50 metres from the free public beach and the sea.
You will be a few steps from all the shops, bars and restaurants in the centre.
The supermarket is less than a 5-minute walk away, and the pharmacy just two minutes.
There is a large car park nearby.

For activities, events, excursions and guest discounts, see our travel guide

Check-in and check-out

Times:
- Check-in from 3:00 pm to 6:00 pm
- Check-out by 11:00 am

Different times must be arranged in advance, subject to availability.

On arrival you will be welcomed in person by one of our hosts, who will look after you throughout your stay.

Please let us know your arrival time in advance.

Complimentary extras

In the bathroom you will find a complimentary toiletry kit with shampoo, hand soap, shower gel and intimate wash.
The products come in small sizes; for longer stays we suggest bringing your own.

A complimentary set of towels (one bath towel, one face towel and one hand towel).

In the kitchen you will find a coffee machine by Krups, compatible with Nescafé Dolce Gusto capsules. We are happy to offer you a few coffees on the house.

Equipped kitchen

The kitchen has:

- 4-burner gas hob
- Electric oven
- Plates, cups, glasses and cutlery
- Pots, pans and utensils
- Fridge and freezer
- Sponge
- Washing-up liquid
- Tablecloth

Washing machine

The apartment has a washing machine, easy to use and at your disposal.

For longer stays, we recommend bringing a few tablets of your favourite detergent from home.

You will also find clothes pegs for hanging out your laundry.
There is no tumble dryer.

Sheets and towels

We provide all the bed linen, together with a complimentary set of towels. If you’d rather not bring extra towels from home, we can supply additional sets on request at laundry cost.
See prices and terms on the services page

- general waste
