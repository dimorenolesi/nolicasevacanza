Noli Panoramic Terrace Apartment

An exclusive penthouse in Noli’s old town, just a five-minute walk from the beach. Its prime position offers breathtaking views over the bay and the medieval village, framed by its distinctive towers and the castle.

The apartment has two large private terraces, perfect for breakfast with a sea view or an aperitivo at sunset, in a truly unique and evocative setting.

Inside you will find elegant, bright rooms, tastefully furnished and equipped with every modern comfort: a refined retreat that blends luxury and authenticity, ideal for anyone who wants to experience Noli from an exclusive vantage point.

Surroundings

Located right in the heart of the village, a few steps from all the shops, bars and restaurants and 200 metres from the beach.

The supermarket and the pharmacy are less than a 5-minute walk away.

The building is inside the limited traffic zone (ZTL), but there is a large car park nearby.
