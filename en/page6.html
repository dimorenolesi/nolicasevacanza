Noli Easy Stay

This newly renovated one-bedroom apartment is in a quiet part of Noli, just a five-minute walk from the old town and the sea. Modern and comfortable, it is ideal for anyone looking for convenience without giving up peace and quiet.

Its real highlight is the reserved parking space in the building’s car park: a rare and valuable perk in Noli, especially in summer, when finding a parking spot can be extremely difficult.

The location is also perfect for nature lovers: several scenic trails through the greenery start just a few steps away, ideal for hikes and restorative walks.

Staying here means enjoying Noli in complete relaxation, with every convenience close at hand.

Surroundings
