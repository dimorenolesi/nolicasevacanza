Holiday homes in Noli

Experience the magic of one of Italy’s most beautiful villages, surrounded by history, the sea and the authentic charm of Liguria.
From practical one-bedroom flats to a luxury penthouse with a view, all our homes have been carefully renovated and furnished to be welcoming, practical and fully equipped. 
Whether you’re looking for a seaside home for several months or just a few days, we have the right place for you.

About us

Behind Dimore Nolesi are Cristina and Daniela: two sisters who run, with enthusiasm and dedication, a family business whose roots go back to the years after the Second World War, when our great-grandmother began welcoming the first travellers to Noli. Since then, the art of hospitality has been handed down from generation to generation, always by the women of our family.

Some of our homes are genuine 11th-century medieval houses, others are in more recent buildings: all of them are lovingly looked after, renovated and constantly improved, because we believe every stay should be not just comfortable, but special.

Our real strength is the joy of hosting: making you feel welcome, pampered and at home, even far from home.
